Microcontroller Wiring & Troubleshooting Guide

Why did code fail in other tools?

  • Adafruit ST7789 requires tft.init(width, height), NOT tft.begin(). Calling begin() throws compiler error or black screen.
  • Adafruit ST7735 requires tft.initR(INITR_BLACKTAB).
  • Adafruit SSD1306 requires display.begin(SSD1306_SWITCHCAPVCC, 0x3C) with Wire.
  • RGB565 Endianness (Byte Swap): In Adafruit_GFX / Wokwi, drawRGBBitmap() expects natural byte order. If "Swap Byte Order" is enabled, bytes get inverted causing crazy rainbow/solarized colors (red becomes dark blue/purple, white becomes green). Keep "Swap Byte Order" unchecked for Adafruit_GFX and Wokwi!
  • MISO Pin Function: MISO (Master In, Slave Out) is only for reading data from display back to ESP32 (e.g. reading touch screen or SD card). It is never used for drawing images and has zero effect on image quality. Quality is determined entirely by resolution and RGB565 dithering.
  • RGB vs BGR Panels: If red and blue colors are swapped on your physical screen, switch the Color Channel Order dropdown to BGR.
